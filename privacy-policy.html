Privacy Policy for Jouleverse

Effective Date: November 10, 2026

Summary: Jouleverse is an interactive 3D physics explainer and educational book published by PRIMESOFT INTERNATIONAL. We believe in total user privacy. Jouleverse does not collect, record, process, or share any personal user data.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Jouleverse operates entirely on your device. We do not collect:

  • Personal identifiable information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • Precise or approximate location data.
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or hardware identifiers.
  • Usage diagnostics, crash logs, or telemetry sent to external servers.
  • Microphone, camera, or sensor recordings.

2. Internet Access and Permissions

Jouleverse runs locally. The application includes standard network access capability solely for local loopback serving of book content and offline caching. No data is transmitted to external servers or third parties.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertising

Jouleverse does not contain advertisements, third-party trackers, or analytics SDKs.

4. Children's Privacy

Jouleverse is an educational app designed for students, educators, and general audiences aged 13 and above. Because we do not collect personal data from any user, we comply with global privacy standards, including COPPA and GDPR.

5. Changes to This Policy

If our privacy practices change, we will update this document and the date at the top of the page.
